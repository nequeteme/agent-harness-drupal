# Content and brand voice

Consolidated knowledge — editorial-flow patterns that have already proven
stable and repeat, so that `content-writer`/`style-reviewer` don't have to
re-derive them every time.

Typical entries here cover things like: the source language content gets
drafted in and how translations are coordinated, whether a tone-of-voice
guide exists yet (and where), and any other recurring editorial convention
worth remembering across tasks. Written by the
[documenter](../../agents/agent-documenter.md) when the
[content-writer](../../agents/agent-content-writer.md) or
[style-reviewer](../../agents/agent-style-reviewer.md) surface something
stable enough to graduate from short-term memory.
